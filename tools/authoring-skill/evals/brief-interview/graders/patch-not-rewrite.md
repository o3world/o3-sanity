`background` and `links` belong to gathering and are already written. A patch
carrying them is a wholesale rewrite of the document wearing an update's
clothes: it restates fields this stage never asked about, from a copy in
context that is one edit out of date the moment Nick corrects anything.

Per-field `set` ops, and only over the fields this stage owns.
