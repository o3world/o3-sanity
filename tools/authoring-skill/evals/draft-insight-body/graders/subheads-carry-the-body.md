One landmark every 150 to 250 words, and the brief asks for about 1,200 — so five
to eight, of which the subheads are most. Four H2s is the floor, low enough that a
body carrying a figure and a pull quote as well still clears the rhythm rule, and
high enough to fail the thing the rule exists to prevent: one long run of prose
with nothing lifted out of it.

H2 and not H3 is deliberate. The three insights written for this site use H2 and
nothing else; the archive's H3s are a WordPress theme's doing.
