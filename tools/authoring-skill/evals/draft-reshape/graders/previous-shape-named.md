The brief carries the previous pass's arc by name. Naming it back — and saying
what the moved thesis does to it, whether that is breaking it or leaving it
standing — is what makes "a different shape" a claim Nick can check in one line
instead of a promise he has to read 1,200 words to test.

The floor, deliberately weaker than `arc-rederived`: this one passes on the name
appearing anywhere in the proposal, so a run that names the previous arc in a
paragraph rather than on its required line fails one grader and not both, and
the report says which of the two went wrong.
