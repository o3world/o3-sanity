---
type: llm
target: last_message
focus: The proposed section list, read against the five-section outline the brief already carries.
criteria: |
  The proposal is a different SHAPE, not the previous outline reordered.

  Score 1 when all three hold:
  - The section list is not a permutation of the previous one. At least two
    sections do something the previous list has no section for, or the piece
    opens on a different move than "grant the boast".
  - The change is driven by the moved thesis. Funding is argued somewhere the
    previous shape had no room for it — the capital-versus-operating split, or
    what the reader asks for and from whom.
  - The grounding ledger was rebuilt rather than copied: at least one section's
    Requires or Grounds names a concept the previous ledger does not.

  Score 0 when the section list is the previous five renamed, reordered, or with
  one appended — a piece whose shape survived a thesis it no longer argues.
---

The whole point of proposing an arc by name is that a second pass is checkable.
This is the check.
