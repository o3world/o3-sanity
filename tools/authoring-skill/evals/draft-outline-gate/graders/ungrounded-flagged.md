Q4 asks how the piece differs from the token-governance advice already
published, and the brief carries no such material — the gap list says nobody
has looked and Nick says he does not have it. That gap is engaged whatever the
section list does, so `none` is not an answer available on this brief.

The pattern refuses `none` and nothing more, which is deliberate: it is the
middle rung of a ladder, and which rung a run falls off says what went wrong.
`ungrounded-reported` says a line exists at all, this one says the line is not a
clean bill of health, and `ungrounded-names-the-gap` says it is about the right
concept ([#203](https://github.com/o3world/o3-sanity/issues/203)).
