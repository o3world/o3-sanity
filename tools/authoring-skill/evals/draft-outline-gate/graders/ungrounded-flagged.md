Q4 asks how the piece differs from the token-governance advice already
published, and the brief carries no such material — the gap list says nobody
has looked and Nick says he does not have it. A section answering Q4 leans on a
concept nothing before it grounds, and no amount of drafting will supply it.
The proposal says so at the gate, where it costs a sentence.

The pattern refuses `none` because `none` is the other legal way to fill the
slot, and on this brief it is the wrong one. A run that narrows the claim to
stay inside what the brief has, then reports a clean check, has taken one of the
three answers the human was supposed to pick between — the failure recorded on
[#203](https://github.com/o3world/o3-sanity/issues/203).
