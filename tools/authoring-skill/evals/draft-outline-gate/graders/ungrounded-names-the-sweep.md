The `UNGROUNDED:` line ends in what would close the concept, and on this brief
the answer is not in doubt. Q4 asks how the piece differs from the
token-governance advice already published; the gap says nobody has looked. That
material exists and is public, so what closes it is a second sweep — gather run
again on the one question, or a cited research file written before the prose.

The two answers this rules out are the expensive ones. Narrowing the thesis to
drop Q4 throws away a question the brief already promised a reader, and asking
Nick is asking the interview for something the interview cannot reach: he has
already said nobody has looked. Naming the sweep costs a clause and leaves the
choice where it belongs, with the human at the gate.
