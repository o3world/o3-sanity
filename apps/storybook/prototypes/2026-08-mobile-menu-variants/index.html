What does the 402 nav open into?

Every canonical page layer has a 1440 and a 402 frame. At 402 the NavBar collapses to a logo, “Let’s talk”, and a 42×42 Open menu control (1814:1636) — and no frame in the file draws the panel behind it. Not on Home mobile, not on Local Components. This is a proposal filling that gap, not a reading of something Figma already says.

Three variants, switchable at the bottom of the page or with ← →. They disagree about three things: how much screen the panel takes, how loud its surface is, and how big the nav copy gets. The closed bar is rebuilt from 1814:1630 and shown beside each one, because two of the three arguments turn on what the bar does while the panel is open.

What all three agree on

The bar never moves

The logo, “Let’s talk” and the control stay exactly where 1814:1630 puts them; the panel fills the 794px below. So the primary CTA is never duplicated and never relocated — the frame already placed it, and a second copy at the bottom of the screen would be the panel inventing a button the design has.

A pleasing consequence of the alpha: rgba(3,3,3,.2) over an ink-deep panel resolves to #030303. The bar dissolves into the panel on open and reappears against the hero on close, with no state of its own.

The control becomes the close

It stays in place and cross-fades to Material close (ADR 0009, already in packages/ui as CloseIcon), drawn 24px wide and right-aligned in the same 42×42 box so it inherits the hamburger’s optical size. It carries aria-label (“Open menu” ↔ “Close menu”), aria-expanded and aria-controls — the exception ADR 0009 flags, because this is the design’s only icon-only control.

Motion, and the absence of it

The surface wipes down from under the bar over --duration-hover (220ms) on --ease-out, and the items rise 12px as they fade. One value here has no token: the 40ms stagger step. Under prefers-reduced-motion the entrance collapses entirely and the open state is byte-identical — no fade, no rise, no delay.

Everything comes from Site Settings

Five items and the CTA, read from the same navItems / primaryCta the 1440 pill uses — Work, Live, Insights (route /perspectives), Solutions, About, and “Let’s talk” → /contact. /live is settled by ADR 0011. No item is added, dropped, or reordered for mobile.

What this does not decide

Whether the CTA belongs in the thumb arc. All three variants leave “Let’s talk” in the bar, at the top of an 874px screen, which is the hardest place on a phone to reach. The sibling capture 2026-08-o3-mobile-menu takes the other side and pins a second copy at the bottom. The two captures now bracket the question deliberately — it is a call for the design owner, not for a prototype.

Whether this becomes a Figma frame, or code becomes the source of record for this one component with the deviation written into docs/figma-components.md. That is #51’s fourth acceptance criterion and the one thing a prototype structurally cannot answer.

Current-route treatment. The 1440 pill has none — no frame draws an active nav item at either width. A declines to invent one; B and C each propose one, so the difference is visible rather than argued.
