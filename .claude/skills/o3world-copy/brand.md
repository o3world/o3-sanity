# O3 World brand foundation

Source material for copy. These are beliefs to _demonstrate_, not sentences to
publish. If a line below appears verbatim in output copy, the copy isn't done —
write what the belief looks like as a specific claim (see SKILL.md, rule 6).

## Positioning

We are our clients' partner in all things experiences. We help them see a
clear path forward.

## Pillars

- **We never settle for the status quo.** We are constantly innovating on our
  work, our own process, and ourselves.
- **We see the ongoing journey.** A circle is a line with no end, and that's
  how we treat relationships.
- **We stay grounded.** Our work is intentional, ties into business goals, and
  pushes the roadmap forward.
- **We plan for the unexpected.** We know how ripples can affect an entire
  ecosystem, and pivot when the plans change.
- **We bring visions to life.** We work with our partners to ideate on new
  concepts, design beautiful experiences, and develop products that evolve as
  the world does. The end of a project is also the beginning of its next
  iteration.

## How we deliver

- We advocate for the user.
- We show up for each other.
- We ensure all voices are heard.
- We communicate with integrity.
- We meet high-quality standards.
- We establish and manage expectations.
- We bring our clients into the process.
- We commit to our expertise and growth.
- We believe more heads are better than one.

## Values

- **Apply optimism.** Be the change you seek by sharing enthusiasm, positivity,
  and good nature one person at a time.
- **Trust in people.** Assume positive intent; treat others the way you'd want
  to be treated; stay open to respectful, constructive dialogue.
- **Commit to community.** Values and culture alignment that promotes
  diversity, equity, and inclusion inside the company and in our communities.
- **Embrace iteration.** Continuous improvement takes flexibility and a
  willingness to grow.
- **Focus on the future.**

## How pillars map to copy

| Belief                  | What it sounds like on the page                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Never settle            | A claim that unsettles: "Most firms ship what you asked for. We solve what was actually in the way."                            |
| Ongoing journey         | Endings framed as starts: "The end of a project is the beginning of its next iteration" → case studies that end on what's next. |
| Stay grounded           | Business consequences, named costs, admitted tradeoffs: "It's a slower way to grow. It's the only way to do work this deep."    |
| Plan for the unexpected | Copy that acknowledges the reader's messy reality (org charts, twelve portals, stakes) instead of a frictionless fantasy.       |
| Bring visions to life   | Concrete deeds with human subjects: "We built the front door that made it feel like one."                                       |
